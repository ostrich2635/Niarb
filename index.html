Why two filter nodes beat flat white noise.

Human speech intelligibility depends on two distinct acoustic regions: the Fundamental & Vowel Formants (600 Hz – 1,800 Hz) which carry vocal warmth, and the Consonant Sibilance Band (2,000 Hz – 3,500 Hz) which distinguishes "p", "t", "k", and "s". NIARB v6.1 lets you manipulate both nodes directly on the CAD spectrum while controlling overall loudness from the top Master Output fader.
